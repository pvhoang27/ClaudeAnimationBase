# "Coffee Loop": a 14.5 s vertical Short (1080×1920)

Why this idea: the animated Shorts that travel furthest right now are meme-style, wordless, instantly relatable
situations, and Shorts reward clips that loop. Everyone knows the morning coffee cycle: zombie → one sip → hyper →
crash → back to zombie. The last frame flows straight back into the first, so the Short loops for real.

Logline: Clawd is a zombie before coffee, but one sip sends Clawd into a hyper frenzy that burns out just as fast, so
Clawd ends exactly where it started, eyeing the mug again.

World: a small kitchen counter under a window. Cool dawn blue-grey at the start (sleepy), a hot ochre/rose
energy burst in the middle (hyper), and back to washed-out blue at the end (crash). bpm 120.

Motif: the mug and its steam. The steam wakes Clawd up; at the end, new steam starts curling up, Clawd's eye cracks open,
and the loop begins again.

Clawd's arc: sleepy → hopeful (smells coffee) → drinking → surprised → excited/starstruck → dizzy → sleepy → one eye opens.

Shots:
  A  0.0–4.5   [in: iris opens on Clawd]  Kitchen counter at dawn. Clawd slumps, asleep, zzz. A mug steams beside Clawd.
               EVENT: the steam drifts to Clawd's nose; Clawd wakes, grabs the mug, gulps it down. Then a beat of
               stillness, and Clawd's eyes snap wide open.
     reads:  0.0–1.4  Clawd is asleep on a counter (iris in, zzz)
             1.4–2.3  steam curls from the mug toward Clawd (eye follows the steam)
             2.3–2.8  Clawd's eyes open and look at the mug (show the thought)
             2.8–3.7  Clawd lifts the mug and gulps it down (mug tilts, gulp squashes)
             3.7–4.0  hold, mug down: nothing... (the beat before the joke)
             4.0–4.5  TAKE: eyes wide, body vibrates → white flash
  B  4.5–9.3   [cut: a white jolt flash, cut under full cover]  An energy-burst background, rays spinning, colour pulsing on
               the beat. Clawd is hyper: vibrating, then hopping, then spinning through the key views with smears.
     reads:  4.5–5.6  Clawd is supercharged (sparkle eyes, gold tint, shaking camera, rays)
             5.6–7.2  hops on the beat, higher each time
             7.2–8.6  spins faster and faster (drawn turns + smears)
             8.6–9.3  peak: a final huge leap off the top of the frame → brush wipe in grey-blue (the crash)
  C  9.3–14.5  [in: the wipe drags off]  Back at the counter, the same framing as A, but the light is cold and the mug
               is empty. Clawd drops in from above and lands, still buzzing, then runs out of battery: dizzy, then slowly
               melts into the same slump as the opening. Payoff: new steam starts curling from the mug and one of Clawd's
               eyes cracks open, looking at it, just as the iris closes.
     reads:  9.3–10.2  Clawd lands back at the counter
             10.2–11.4 dizzy (swirl eyes, wobble)
             11.4–12.8 battery drains: slow melt into the sleepy slump, zzz
             12.8–13.6 new steam rises from the mug (the eye goes to the mug)
             13.6–14.5 one eye opens toward it → iris closes on Clawd (the loop restarts with the iris opening)
  [out: iris closes to black, which matches the iris opening at 0.0]
